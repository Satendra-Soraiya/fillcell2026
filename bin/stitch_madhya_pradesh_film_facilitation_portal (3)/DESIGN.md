## Brand & Style
The Madhya Pradesh Film Facilitation Cell bridges state-level governance with the dynamic energy of world-class cinema. It serves filmmakers, production houses, creative directors, and cultural stakeholders seeking access to Madhya Pradesh's diverse natural and heritage shooting locations, financial incentives, and streamlined single-window clearances.

The aesthetic fuses **Cinematic Grandeur** with **Modern Sovereign Governance** in a high-luxury Dark Palette: **Obsidian, Charcoal, Gold, and Amber**. It commands attention with rich black glass surfaces, gold metallic highlights, warm ambient lighting, and monumental typography. Surfaces are deep, layered, and moody, allowing high-resolution production photography of palaces, fortresses, and landscapes to glow with cinematic intensity. The emotional tone is prestigious, authoritative, majestic, and world-class.

## Colors & Visual Combination
The visual combination is defined by:
- **Obsidian (`#050508` to `#09090D`)**: Deep sovereign black forming the primary stage and high-contrast backdrop.
- **Charcoal (`#12121A` to `#171722`)**: Elevated glass surfaces, container cards, and structural panels with subtle borders.
- **Radiant Gold (`#FBBF24` / `#F59E0B`)**: Precious metal accents for typography highlights, badges, award ribbons, and glowing indicators.
- **Amber & Sunset Orange (`#F97316` / `#EA580C`)**: High-energy CTA buttons, action pills, interactive states, and gradient bridges.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Ultra-subtle light borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) paired with gold border glow (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`).

## Typography
Montserrat is specified across all display, narrative, technical, and interface levels. Rooted in geometric construction, Montserrat mirrors the bold, monumental typography of the official emblem ("FILM FACILITATION CELL") while offering exceptional readability across administrative portals and location catalogs.

Display headings utilize heavier weights (`700`, `800`, `900`) with tight letter spacing for commanding titles and cinema-inspired headings. UI labels, metadata tags, and government status trackers leverage uppercase styling with extended tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to mirror official credentialing and production clapperboard semantics. Body copy remains at normal letter spacing in medium-regular weights in crisp silver-gray (`#CBD5E1` and `#F8FAFC`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs an 8-point base rhythm across a responsive 12-column grid system (desktop), transitioning to an 8-column layout (tablet), and a 4-column layout (mobile).

Consistent spatial tiers (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) preserve clean breathing space around dense documentation tables, location picture galleries, and application submission steppers.

## Elevation & Depth (Dark Luxury)
- **Level 0 (Obsidian Stage)**: `#050508` and `#09090D`. The deepest background canvas.
- **Level 1 (Charcoal Planes)**: `#12121A` with subtle glass borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Elevated Glowing Cards)**: Hovered location cards, permit summary tiles, and active form steps with golden glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Glass Modals)**: Navigation header and modals featuring 85% opacity dark obsidian with backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-xl`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Glowing gradient button (`from-amber-500 via-orange-500 to-amber-600`), bold black typography, rounded-xl (12px), with ambient gold glow shadow.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Translucent dark glass (`bg-white/10 hover:bg-white/15`) with subtle border (`border-white/20 hover:border-amber-400/60`) and crisp white text.

### Cards & Media Panels
- Elevated charcoal backgrounds (`#12121A`), 16px corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), subtle `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders, and 16:9 cinematic aspect-ratio thumbnail headers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Capsule-shaped (`rounded-full`), font size 11px, bold uppercase. Clearances feature gold glass fills (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`) with gold borders and text.
- **Location Filter Chips**: Dark slate fill (`#151522`) with gold/amber active states.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), 8px border radius, and 12px/16px interior padding.
- Focused state applies a 2px outline in vibrant Sunset Orange (`#EA580C`) with a subtle `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)` box-shadow ring. Labels use `Montserrat` 600 in uppercase format with micro-tracking.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Clean alternating or bordered rows with subtle `#FAFAF9` hovers. Headers in bold charcoal (`#111827`) with uppercase tracking. Cell padding aligned to 16px vertical rhythm.

### Government Clearance Stepper (Specialized)
- A linear progress tracker for permit clearance stages featuring numbered milestone nodes linked by a 2px track that illuminates from sunset orange to golden amber as approvals finalize.
